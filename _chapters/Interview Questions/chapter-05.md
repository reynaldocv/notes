## Some Questions

### "Tell me about a time you made an ordering mistake or faced a major inventory glitch.

- **Situation**: At my previous retail job, I accidentally ordered double the seasonal stock for a high-demand item and fell short on another key product. 

- **Task**: I needed to fix the inventory mismatch before it affected customer satisfaction and caused a financial loss for the store. 

- **Action**: I talked to my manager immediately, devised a plan to return the surplus items to the vendor, and arranged a minor promotional discount for the shortage items to balance out sales. 

- **Result**: We avoided a major financial loss, turned a small profit on the adjusted items, and my boss commended my proactive approach. Moving forward, I instituted a double-check system and recurring reminders for future orders.
