# Baires Resume 

Hello, how are you all doing?

My name is Reynaldo Caceres, and I am from Peru.

I studied software engineering there, then worked in web development for a year, and later moved to Brazil to pursue a master’s degree in computer science.

I returned to Peru and worked in software development until 2018. I then went back to Brazil to complete a PhD in computer science. Over the last two years, I have worked with big data and repository management.

I have about 7 years of programming experience.

I have programmed in Java, C++, PHP, Python, and other languages.

I also have 8 years of research experience (from my master’s and PhD studies, which focused on data security)
at the Institute of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of São Paulo.

I would like to work with you for three reasons: I want to work as a Python programmer, I want to improve my English, and I want to work remotely.

That’s all—thank you very much for listening and for this opportunity.
